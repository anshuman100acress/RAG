## Opening Hours
Riverside GP Surgery is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 6:30 PM.
We are closed on weekends and UK bank holidays. For urgent non-emergency care out of hours, call NHS 111.

## Repeat Prescriptions
Repeat prescriptions take 48 working hours to process.
Patients can request them via the NHS App or by dropping the paper slip in the reception box. We do not accept prescription requests over the phone for safety reasons.

## Booking Appointments
Routine GP appointments can be booked up to 2 weeks in advance.
Same-day urgent appointments are released at 8:00 AM daily.

## Test Results
Blood test results usually take 5 to 7 working days.
Please call after 2:00 PM to check your results, or view them directly in the NHS App.
